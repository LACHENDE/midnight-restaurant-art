# まよなか屋 Midnight Restaurant: ChatGPT art prompts

Make all 36 pictures in one ChatGPT chat so they share a style. Send the style message first with your target picture attached. Then, for each picture, attach its sketch from `ref/` and paste its prompt. Upload what ChatGPT makes to this repo (any file names).

## Style message (send first, with your target picture attached)

I'm making a cozy two-player ramen-stall game called Midnight Restaurant, and I'll ask you for 36 game pictures, one per message. The picture attached here is my style target: match it in every picture.

Style: chunky, rounded, toy-like shapes, like soft vinyl toys or smooth clay, with simple forms and not too many details. Warm, soft light from orange paper lanterns, gentle shading and soft highlights, a cozy rainy night in Japan. Colours: warm wood browns, lantern orange, noren red, deep navy night, cream, and a little mint green for small accents only.

Camera: from the front and a little above, like a side-view game, so round things such as bowls, pots and cups show an oval top.

Rules for every picture:
- No text, letters, kana, numbers or logos anywhere, and no watermark.
- For an object or a character: only that one thing, centred, whole, with space around it, on a transparent background. No table, no floor, no cast shadow.
- For a scene: fill the whole picture, with no people in it.
- Keep the same style, lighting and level of detail in every picture of this chat.

With each request I'll attach a rough sketch from the current version of the game. Use it only for the shape, the angle and where things go, never for the style. Reply "ready" and wait for my first request.
